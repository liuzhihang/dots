# Visual review of native homepage captures — v76

final result: blocked

This is an interim review of the final v76 capture set, not an approval of the character art and not the final `design-qa.md`. The source ID recorded by the native capture is `d63ba9b103b983cf39f298e9e5e38119a47b053fe752f1b53c53453e511d49d6`; `pages-v76.json` identifies fresh tab15 native screenshots with all five family cards and both hero groups loaded. I compared the provided R01 image, A02, `home-{1280,768,390,320}-v76.png`, and `cards-{390,320}-v76.png`. I did not use the invalid tab14 or v72 captures.

## P2 blockers

1. **The family-card images do not preserve the R01 group compositions closely enough.** R01 uses distinct, deliberately staged sizes and overlaps: Dots has a large headphone orb with a small yellow companion behind and a pink triangle in front; Grok uses six different geometric silhouettes in a clear top/back-to-front arrangement; Humans has three larger, tightly grouped children; Animals has a compact multi-species group. In v76, the Dots card instead presents a blue headphone orb, mint ball, pink glasses ball, lilac bucket-hat ball and peach ball, changing both the cast and accessory hierarchy. The Grok card keeps six colorful shapes but centers a large purple sphere and moves the green cloud to the right; its silhouette hierarchy differs from R01. The Humans card keeps three original characters but makes the group visibly smaller and changes the left and right figures’ hair/clothing read. The Animals card also changes the visible species mix and relative placement. These differences are especially visible at 1280 and remain in the 390/320 card captures.

2. **Card artwork occupies less of the card than in R01, and the small English captions lose the reference’s deliberate two-line hierarchy.** At 1280 the R01 groups fill more of each card’s lower image area; v76 leaves more open space around the groups, particularly for Humans and Grok. The Dots footer is compressed to a small single-line treatment rather than the reference’s stacked “Simple / But Special.” Similar caption wrapping and scale differences recur across the cards. These are noticeable at desktop and become harder to read in the narrow mobile cards.

3. **The Muse card does not yet make the requested A02 identity legible.** A02’s identity cues are a thick ivory hood, warm face, short limbs and fine plush surface. The v76 Muse card reads as a cream-and-pink pair with a daisy/flower prop, rather than a clearly identifiable ivory hood character holding the specified star with visible hand-to-star contact. The cream figure in the hero shares the hooded plush direction, but the captured size does not let me verify the star/contact detail. This needs to be checked against the authorized Muse adjustment in the replacement capture.

No P0/P1 defect is established by these screenshots. The R01-like pastel palette, centered hero title, paired hero groups, five-card structure, and no-login/no-user-space navigation are present. The 390/320 card captures show the Animals card with text left and image right. The v76 viewport images do not support the earlier claim of definite mobile edge-cropping. Layout/asset presence is not an art acceptance signal.

## Not evaluated here

The intro-specific v76 captures and `interaction-record-v76.json` were not present when this review was made available, so I make no interaction or below-the-fold intro acceptance claim. User confirmation of the five family artworks remains outstanding. The eventual `design-qa.md` must be based on the replacement capture set and must preserve that art-approval boundary.
